## World and interface concept

Saved image: [exploration-worlds-v1.png](exploration-worlds-v1.png).

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original art-direction sheet for a small spherical-world exploration game; speculative target art, not a screenshot of an existing implementation.
Primary request: walking and exploring distinctive worlds, with crafting supporting the journey. Make an attractive, achievable stylized 3D game look with memorable ecological shapes, tactile surface variation and an understated field-journal interface.
Composition: landscape concept sheet. One large main view occupies about 70 percent: third-person walking camera just behind a modest explorer, eye level above a gently curved shore path leading into a grove. Ground is made of broad irregular hexagonal terrain terraces, softened at the edges, with readable paths and exposed rock faces; curvature is gentle at walking scale. A huge contrasting planet is visible in a hazy sky, grounded in the game's many-world premise. Habitats have layered canopy, sparse understory, meadow and damp shoreline, with deliberate open spaces. Two small unfamiliar grazing animals have believable weight and simple leg anatomy. A distinctive natural stone landmark draws exploration. To the right, two smaller game-camera studies: an amber canyon habitat with wind-shaped dry plants and a blue dusk fungal habitat with restrained warm bioluminescence. All views are plausible real-time low-to-mid-poly game art, broad deliberate faceted forms with baked detail and careful light, not cinematic photorealism.
Character: friendly compact adult explorer in a practical cloth suit, warm ivory fabric, muted moss accents and a small ochre field pack, visible articulated boots and gloves, rounded protective headgear, no recognizable franchise design.
Material direction: color and roughness variation at broad and fine scales, matte warm rock, subtle grass and moss grain, small wet edge highlights; avoid gritty screen noise and uniform plastic. Sunlight describes form and routes; color has intentional contrast.
Interface only on main view: small quiet upper-left title "AURELIA", below it "Shoreline grove"; minimal lower-left expedition compass with destination "Grove"; a small lower-right prompt "Observe". Warm ivory serif and compact readable sans serif, fine charcoal translucent backing, generously unobstructed world view.
Text: small sheet caption "EXPLORATION / CONCEPT TARGET". No other copy.
Avoid: dense HUD, neon dashboards, generic spaceship cockpit, crafting grids, survival meter wall, oversaturated rainbow, Minecraft blocks, giant featureless spheres, spikes covering every surface, overly elaborate assets that cannot read at game scale.
```

## Explorer and movement concept

Saved image: [explorer-movement-v1.png](explorer-movement-v1.png).

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original player-character and movement concept sheet for a spherical-world exploration game. This is a visual brief for a complete mesh, skeleton, rig and animation rebuild, not a technical rig proof.
Primary request: design a distinctive and appealing explorer who walks, climbs and observes wildlife on small alien worlds. Show an achievable stylized game asset with readable joints and grounded locomotion.
Composition: landscape sheet on warm off-white paper with restrained charcoal labels. Left half: consistent front, side and back orthographic character views in a relaxed A-pose, same proportions and costume. Right half: four full-body expressive movement studies, labeled "Walk", "Step up", "Observe", "Reach". Feet in the walking and stepping studies visibly contact sloped hexagonal stone surfaces; one reaching pose shows a hand gripping a ledge. Small bottom inset: the same character seen from the third-person gameplay camera against a grove silhouette, to demonstrate legibility at play distance.
Character: adult proportions stylized toward a compact strong silhouette, approximately five heads tall, practical warm ivory cloth suit with muted moss-green panels, compact ochre field pack and rolled specimen pouch, dark flexible joints, reinforced articulated boots and expressive gloved hands. A rounded open protective headgear frame with a simple clearly visible face, approachable explorer identity without a militarized or space-marine look. The silhouette comes from a thoughtful asymmetrical field pack, useful clothing and recognizable stance, not tiny greebles.
Materials: broad low-to-mid-poly game-ready forms, restrained textile grain, soft matte painted hardware, clean roughness separation. Show elbows, knees, wrists, ankles, shoulder and hip bending areas clearly; costume pieces allow deformation. Anatomy consistent across views. Belt attachments and backpack rigid enough for simple secondary motion.
Small labels exactly: "EXPLORER / CONCEPT TARGET", "Front", "Side", "Back", "Walk", "Step up", "Observe", "Reach". Optional thin dotted lines near boots communicate ground contact, without fake technical specifications or bone-count claims.
Style: polished original game concept art presented as a useful production design sheet, not photorealism, not toy packaging, no recognizable franchise styling.
Avoid: stiff primitive sphere body, giant armor plates, full face obscured by black visor, excessive dangling gear, tiny illegible technical diagrams, neon trim, weapons, robotic limbs, inconsistent outfits, extra fingers or limbs.
```
